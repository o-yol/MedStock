MedStock

Alarms ring only while this page is open in a browser tab. If you close the tab, they stop.

No medicines to show. Press "Add medicine" to start.

Add medicine

Add medicines from a photo

Choose or take a photo of a medicine strip, box or printed prescription. The photo stays on this device. The reading tool and its English language data are downloaded from the internet the first time you use it.

This sends the photo to Google's servers.

Reading can be wrong, especially handwriting. Always check the name against the prescription.

Settings

The "Send WhatsApp reminder" button opens WhatsApp with a message ready. You must press Send yourself. It is not automatic.

Warning: the key stays on this device only, saved in this browser. Anyone who can open this browser on this device could see it. Never share it.

Notifications

Phone alarms (work even when this app is closed)

Open the downloaded file on your phone or import it into a calendar. Some calendars (Google Calendar especially) may ignore the alarm inside the file, so always test it. The steps are in the README.

Backup

Restoring replaces the medicines on this device. The backup does not include the Gemini key or the WhatsApp number.

This app tracks stock and reminders only. It does not give medical advice. Follow the doctor's prescription.
